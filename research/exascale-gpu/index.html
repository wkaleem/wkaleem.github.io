Optimizing complex systems on exascale and GPU platforms

I develop parallel algorithms for large mixed-integer optimization problems, with work on charging-network design, GPU-accelerated primal heuristics and railroad blocking.

Selected research

  • Extreme-scale EV charging infrastructure planning for last-mile delivery using high-performance parallel computing

    Transportation Research Part B, 2026.

    Lagrangian decomposition and rounding heuristics produced feasible network plans for nearly half a million households and 2,000 candidate stations in Chicago within six hours, using 680 CPU cores.

Ongoing work

  • GPU-accelerated primal heuristics

    A collaborative project that grew out of the 2026 Land–Doig MIP Competition. I am the lead developer, implementing GPU kernels in Julia using CUDA.jl.

  • GPU-accelerated neighborhood search for railroad blocking

    Experimental very large-scale neighborhood search based on classical algorithms. I am the lead developer, implementing GPU kernels in C++ using Kokkos.

Software and training

  • NERSC HPC Crash Course, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory; Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing, 2026.
