AI-integrated discrete optimization in transportation and logistics

I develop neural surrogates of operational costs and embed them within mixed-integer planning models, with applications in location-routing and last-mile delivery.

Selected research

  • Neural embedded mixed-integer optimization for location-routing problems

    Under review at Transportation Research Part E, 2026. Also presented at the NeurIPS DLCA Workshop, 2025. Code and data.

    A permutation-invariant neural model estimates routing costs for location-allocation decisions. Across four benchmark sets, solutions are within several percent of the best known; on large instances, the method is more than an order of magnitude faster than existing heuristics.

  • Neural embedded optimization for integrated location and routing problems

    IISE Annual Conference Proceedings, 2024. Best Paper Award, IISE Logistics and Supply Chain Division.

  • Permutation invariant neural estimators for vehicle routing costs

    Submitted, 2026.

    Neural estimators for five routing variants, with prediction errors two- to fifteen-fold smaller than those of the best baselines.

  • Data-driven on-time last-mile delivery

    Presented at the MIP Workshop, 2026.

    Learning batch delivery times for order assignment reduced late deliveries threefold relative to a routing-based baseline on two months of Shanghai food-delivery data.

Ongoing work

Neural embedded optimization for multi-depot, two-echelon and periodic routing, and a survey of optimization with feedforward neural network constraints.
