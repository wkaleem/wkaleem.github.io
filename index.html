Research

I build models and algorithms that place trained machine learning models inside large-scale discrete optimization, so that planning, routing, and pricing decisions can be made at the size real networks reach. Three directions:

  • Matching, scheduling, and pricing in freight marketsApproximate dynamic programming for truckload markets, where a broker clears loads against carriers and learns what capacity is worth as demand arrives.
  • AI-integrated discrete optimization at scaleNeural surrogates embedded as constraints in mixed-integer programs, with decomposition and parallel methods that solve the result on real instances.
  • Electrified freight and last-mile deliveryCharging infrastructure planning and depot scheduling under uncertainty, from facility siting to grid-connected charge schedules.
