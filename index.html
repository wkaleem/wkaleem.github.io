My research develops models, algorithms, and software for decision-making in supply-chain planning, freight and logistics operations, and critical transportation infrastructure. I combine AI-integrated discrete optimization and sequential decision-making under uncertainty with high-performance and GPU computing. I am advised by Anirudh Subramanyam and have worked at Argonne National Laboratory and Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories.
