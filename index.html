About

I am a PhD candidate in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at Penn State, advised by Anirudh Subramanyam. My dissertation, Scalable AI-integrated optimization for electrified transportation and logistics, builds models and algorithms that put trained machine learning models inside large-scale discrete optimization, so that planning, routing, and pricing decisions can be made at the size real freight networks reach.

Parts of that work were done at Argonne National Laboratory, where I have held two summer appointments, and at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories, where research on grid-connected charging schedules led to a joint patent filing.

My work has appeared in Transportation Research Part B and Transportation Research Part C, at the NeurIPS Workshop on Differentiable Learning of Combinatorial Algorithms, and in the IISE Annual Conference Proceedings, where it received the Best Paper Award of the Logistics and Supply Chain Division.

I was selected for the Argonne Training Program on Extreme-Scale Computing, named an IISE Future Faculty Fellow, and reached the final ten for the Lee Schipper Memorial Scholarship of the Volvo Research and Educational Foundations.
